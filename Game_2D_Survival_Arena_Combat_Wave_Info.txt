Game scale: PLAYER DEFAULT = 20 HP
Purpose: Design reference for PBL 2D Survival Arena

============================================================
2. PLAYER
============================================================

PLAYER BASE STATS
- Max HP: 20
- Starting HP: 20
- Movement Speed: 100% (base)
- Attack: determined by equipped weapon
- Defense: determined by armor/items
- Critical Hit: optional
- Knockback Resistance: optional
- Invincibility after taking damage: recommended 0.3-0.5 sec

PLAYER COMBAT
- Basic attack
- Weapon attack
- Skill attack
- Dodge / movement
- Pickup items
- Potion/consumable use

Recommended damage feedback:
- Flash player sprite when hit
- Show floating damage number
- Play hit sound
- Small knockback
- Brief invulnerability frame

============================================================
3. ENEMY ROLE SYSTEM
============================================================

Enemy roles:

1. BASIC
   - Simple melee enemy
   - Low HP
   - Low damage
   - Main purpose: pressure player

2. RANGED
   - Attacks from distance
   - Low/medium HP
   - Forces player to move

3. MELEE DPS
   - High contact damage
   - Medium HP
   - Main threat when close

4. TANK
   - Very high HP
   - High damage
   - Slow
   - Creates pressure and blocks movement

5. SUPPORT
   - Low/medium direct damage
   - Heals/buffs/debuffs
   - Should be prioritized by player

6. MAGIC / ELITE
   - Special attacks
   - Summoning
   - Area attacks
   - Higher HP

7. SUMMON
   - Created by Elite/Magic enemies
   - Shorter HP
   - High mobility or special attack

============================================================
4. MINECRAFT-INSPIRED ENEMIES
============================================================

A. PILLAGER
Role:
- Ranged DPS

Minecraft reference:
- HP: 24
- Main weapon: Crossbow
- Hard ranged damage: about 4.5 HP

2D GAME DESIGN:
- HP: 10
- Damage: 2
- Attack type: projectile
- Range: long
- Movement Speed: 70% of Player
- Attack Cooldown: 1.8-2.5 sec
- Projectile Speed: medium/high

Behavior:
- Keep distance from Player
- Shoot projectile
- Retreat when Player gets too close
- Can move around obstacles if AI supports pathfinding

Priority:
- Medium

Suggested implementation:
Pillager = ranged enemy that makes the player move.

------------------------------------------------------------

B. VINDICATOR
Role:
- Melee DPS

Minecraft reference:
- HP: 24
- Weapon: Iron Axe
- Hard damage: about 19.5 HP

2D GAME DESIGN:
- HP: 10
- Damage: 5
- Attack type: melee
- Range: short
- Movement Speed: 115% of Player
- Attack Cooldown: 1.0-1.5 sec

Behavior:
- Aggressively chase Player
- High contact/attack damage
- No ranged attack

Priority:
- HIGH

Design purpose:
- Punishes Player for standing still
- Forces dodge/kite gameplay

------------------------------------------------------------

C. RAVAGER
Role:
- Tank / Heavy enemy

Minecraft reference:
- HP: 100
- Hard melee damage: about 18
- Roar damage: about 9
- High knockback resistance

2D GAME DESIGN:
- HP: 40
- Melee Damage: 5
- Roar Damage: 3
- Movement Speed: 55% of Player
- Knockback Resistance: 75%
- Attack Cooldown: 2.0 sec
- Roar Cooldown: 6-10 sec

Abilities:

1. CHARGE
   - Ravager moves rapidly toward Player
   - On hit: damage + strong knockback
   - Recommended charge damage: 5

2. ROAR
   - Short/medium area attack
   - Damage: 3
   - Strong knockback
   - Cooldown: 6-10 sec

Priority:
- VERY HIGH

Design purpose:
- Tank enemy
- Controls space
- Punishes Player positioning

------------------------------------------------------------

D. WITCH
Role:
- Support / Debuffer

Minecraft reference:
- HP: 26
- Uses Splash Potions
- Supports Raiders with healing

2D GAME DESIGN:
- HP: 13
- Direct Harming Damage: 3
- Movement Speed: 75% of Player
- Attack Cooldown: 3-4 sec

WITCH POTIONS

1. HARMING
   - Instant damage
   - Damage: 3 HP
   - No damage-over-time

2. POISON
   - Damage over time
   - Damage: 1 HP every 1.25 sec
   - Recommended game duration: 5 sec
   - Total maximum damage: 4 HP
   - Does not need to kill Player directly

3. SLOWNESS
   - Movement speed: -15%
   - Recommended game duration: 3-5 sec
   - Example:
     Base speed 100%
     -> Player speed 85%

4. WEAKNESS
   - Player attack damage: -4
   - Recommended duration: 5 sec

5. REGENERATION (ALLY)
   - Heals nearby Raider
   - Recommended healing: +1 HP every 2.5 sec
   - Maximum recommended duration: 5 sec
   - Maximum heal per application: +2 HP

6. INSTANT HEALTH (ALLY)
   - Instant heal
   - Recommended heal: +4 HP
   - Target: damaged nearby Raider

WITCH AI:
- Avoid Player
- Stay behind other enemies
- Heal damaged elite/tank enemies
- Debuff Player
- Use Harming when appropriate
- Do NOT constantly spam potions

Priority:
- VERY HIGH

Design purpose:
- Support enemy
- Makes other enemies more dangerous
- Encourages target prioritization

------------------------------------------------------------

E. EVOKER
Role:
- Magic / Elite / Summoner

Minecraft reference:
- HP: 24
- Fang attack: about 6 HP
- Can summon Vex

2D GAME DESIGN:
- HP: 15
- Fang/AoE Damage: 3
- Movement Speed: 65% of Player
- Ability Cooldown: 5-8 sec

ABILITY 1: MAGIC FANG
- Spawn a short line/zone of magical spikes
- Damage: 3 HP
- Telegraph before attack: recommended 0.5-1 sec

ABILITY 2: SUMMON VEX
- Summon 1-3 Vex
- Cooldown: 8-12 sec
- Maximum active Vex per Evoker: recommended 3

Priority:
- EXTREMELY HIGH

Design purpose:
- Elite enemy
- Forces Player to manage both Evoker and summons

------------------------------------------------------------

F. VEX
Role:
- Summoned melee enemy

Minecraft reference:
- Small flying hostile mob
- Hard attack damage: about 13.5 HP

2D GAME DESIGN:
- HP: 5
- Damage: 3
- Movement Speed: 140% of Player
- Attack Cooldown: 1.2 sec
- Flying movement
- Can pass through obstacles if desired

Behavior:
- Chase Player aggressively
- Short attack range
- Disappear after a limited lifetime

Recommended lifetime:
- 10-15 sec

Priority:
- HIGH when many Vex are active

Design purpose:
- Fast pressure unit
- Makes Evoker dangerous

8. BOSS DESIGN
============================================================

Boss should NOT simply be a Ravager with more HP.

Recommended:
- Boss HP: 150-200
- Multiple phases
- Telegraph attacks
- Area attacks
- Summon minions
- Enrage at 50% HP
- Reward after defeat

Example:
BOSS HP = 180

Phase 1:
100%-70%
- Melee
- Charge

Phase 2:
70%-40%
- Area attack
- Summon enemies

Phase 3:
40%-0%
- Faster attacks
- More damage
- Enrage
- More summons

============================================================
9. DAMAGE RULES FOR A 20 HP PLAYER
============================================================

Recommended damage tiers:

1 DAMAGE:
- Very weak attack
- Small projectile
- Minor DoT tick

2 DAMAGE:
- Normal ranged attack
- Basic enemy attack

3 DAMAGE:
- Strong ability
- Witch Harming
- Vex attack

4 DAMAGE:
- Elite attack
- Strong special

5 DAMAGE:
- Vindicator / Ravager heavy attack
- Dangerous melee

8+ DAMAGE:
- Boss ultimate
- Should have a clear warning/telegraph

Avoid attacks that instantly kill a 20 HP Player
unless they are explicitly designed as dodgeable boss mechanics.

10. COMBAT PRIORITY
============================================================

Recommended AI target priority:

Witch
    ↓
Evoker
    ↓
Vex
    ↓
Vindicator
    ↓
Pillager
    ↓
Ravager

However, Player should be able to choose freely.

Reason:
- Witch keeps enemies alive
- Evoker creates Vex
- Vex rapidly pressures Player
- Vindicator deals heavy melee damage
- Pillager creates ranged pressure
- Ravager is slow but tanky

============================================================
12. IMPORTANT BALANCING PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Minecraft values are REFERENCE values.

The 2D game should use:
- Player = 20 HP
- Enemy values scaled around that HP
- Difficulty increases through enemy combinations
- Not only through enemy quantity

Progression:
Wave 1 = learn basic enemy
Wave 2 = ranged + melee
Wave 3 = tank introduced
Wave 4 = support introduced
Wave 5 = elite introduced
Wave 6 = summon introduced
Wave 7 = combined final encounter

The goal is:
Each wave introduces or combines a mechanic.
The player should learn the mechanic before being forced
to deal with several mechanics simultaneously.
